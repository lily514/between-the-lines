  1. ✓
    Decided: companion to your TV
    The match plays on a separate stream and never pauses. Questions keep "the match carries on, on your TV". Matches has an "In step with your TV?" ±10 s control; tapping the clock in the live header opens it too.
  2. ✓
    Decided: Matches is home base
    First open goes Welcome → Pick team → Matches. Return visits and "Done for this match" land there: next match, rejoin a live one, progress, earlier reports.
  3. ✓
    Decided: the map from anywhere
    A map button sits in the header of every screen after onboarding, next to ⚙ or ✕. The map opens as a sheet and Back returns you to where you were. Mid-question, the question is saved for the break.
  4. ?
    Confirm: one question per stoppage
    At a throw-in, the live basics question wins and the saved review waits for the next long stoppage or halftime. Never two sheets back to back.
  5. ?
    Confirm: catch-up after answering
    After "Back to the match", the newest caption starts "While you were answering:" and says what happened, so nothing is missed.
  6. ?
    Confirm: a question near kick-off or the whistle
    No live question in the last 30 s before halftime or full time; it becomes one of the report's replays instead.
